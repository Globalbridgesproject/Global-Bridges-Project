Why Global Bridges?

Connection begins
with conversation.

The Global Bridges Project is a youth-led initiative founded by Naser Al-Qatami to connect young people across countries and communities.

Through conversations with people from different backgrounds, we create opportunities to exchange perspectives, explore cultures, and develop a broader understanding of the world.

We believe meaningful conversations can bridge differences, challenge assumptions, and help young people see the world through new perspectives.

Global Bridges is built around a simple belief: listening to someone else's perspective can change the way we understand our own.

We welcome students, curious thinkers, and young leaders who want to share their experiences and learn from others around the world.
